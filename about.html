About the directory

About DevSpire Nexus

DevSpire Nexus is the AI Intelligence Directory from DevSpireHub.com, a static, evidence-oriented catalog of AI tools, models, and developer resources. Discover. Connect. Build.

Registries

REGISTRY::01 403 AI Tools Assistants, coding environments, research engines, media tools, and business automation. REGISTRY::02 300 AI Models Language, reasoning, coding, multimodal, image, audio, video, and embedding models. REGISTRY::03 300 Developer Resources Serving engines, vector databases, agent frameworks, local runtimes, evaluation, and security.

What this directory is

DevSpire Nexus is a browsable intelligence layer for the AI ecosystem. Each registry record keeps the fields the source states (such as licensing, pricing model, deployment format, context window, hosting, and evidence links) instead of inventing values to fill a table.

The catalog is static. Records are prepared from the DevSpireHub AI knowledge base, reviewed in verification passes, and published with a last-verified date. It is not a live price feed, a benchmark leaderboard, or an automated ranking of products.

Search, filters, sorting, deep links, and cross-registry related resources run entirely in the browser. There are no accounts and no server-side profile. Personal collections are stored only in your browser and can be exported as JSON.

You can also access the same catalog through static JSON files documented on the API and Data Access page, or propose a record through the submission workflow.

updateNew resources and features will be introduced as the directory evolves.

How records are prepared

  1. 01Source first. A record starts from the uploaded registry dataset. Fields absent in the source stay absent; they are never guessed.
  2. 02Evidence links. Where the source provides an official page or repository, the record links to it so you can check the claim yourself.
  3. 03Explicit dates. Verification dates are shown with records and registries. A date describes the last review pass, not a promise of live accuracy.
  4. 04Corrections. If a record is wrong or stale, send the record name, URL, and a public evidence link through the contact page. Corrections are reviewed manually.

What it deliberately does not do

blockNo paid placements, scores, or engagement ranking.
blockNo user reviews or testimonials presented as facts.
blockNo automated publishing of submitted records.
blockNo tracking scripts or advertising cookies of our own.
