Privacy Policy

This policy describes the data practices of the DevSpire Nexus website. Last updated November 5, 2026.

no_accounts

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for and no server-side visitor profile.

visibility_off

No tracking scripts

The site ships no analytics, advertising, or session-recording scripts of its own.

bookmark

Local collections

Bookmarks and collections stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

send

Forms are explicit

Contact and submission forms transmit only when a delivery key is configured.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the static DevSpire Nexus pages: the directory landing page, the three registries, documentation, API reference, submission and contact forms, legal pages, and personal collections. It does not govern external websites reached through outbound links, including DevSpireHub.com and the official pages of cataloged products.

2. Information stored in your browser

Personal collections use the browser's localStorage under the key devspire-nexus:personal-collections:v1. The stored payload contains a schema version, bookmarks (resource type, resource ID, and save time), and collections (name, timestamps, and resource references). It contains no account identity, contact details, or free-text notes.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to DevSpireHub, DevSpire Nexus maintainers, or any third party. You can export it as JSON, import a backup, or delete it from the collections page or your browser's site-data settings.

If stored data is malformed, the site keeps an untouched copy under a :corrupt: suffixed key before falling back to an empty state, so a corrupt payload is not silently discarded. The submission form also keeps a short-lived local duplicate fingerprint under dsh-submission: keys to block accidental repeat submissions for the configured window; it contains a hash-like string, not your message.

3. Information this site does not collect

  • No user accounts, passwords, or profiles.
  • No analytics, advertising, heatmap, or session-recording scripts of the site's own.
  • No cookies set by DevSpire Nexus pages for tracking or personalisation.
  • No server-side storage of bookmarks or collections.
  • No sale or rental of personal information, because none is collected by the static pages.

4. Contact and submission forms

The contact form requests a name, email address, topic, and message. The resource submission form requests record details plus optional submitter name and email. Both forms validate input in the browser first.

Delivery uses Web3Forms, a third-party form relay. If the deployer has configured a Web3Forms access key, the form sends the fields you entered to the configured endpoint so the service can forward them to the directory maintainers. Web3Forms processes the request under its own privacy policy and may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the message.

This repository ships with an empty access key. In that state the forms remain visible, validate locally, and state clearly that nothing is transmitted. No message is queued or stored elsewhere.

Both forms include a hidden honeypot field and a minimum completion time to reduce automated spam. The contact form keeps no server-side copy; the submission form keeps a local duplicate fingerprint for a limited window, as described above.

5. Third-party requests

The pages load open-source presentation dependencies from public content networks: the Tailwind CSS CDN, Google Fonts (Geist and JetBrains Mono), and Google's Material Symbols font. Your browser connects directly to those providers, which may receive standard request data such as your IP address, user agent, and the page that requested the asset, under their own policies.

Catalog icons are stored locally in assets/img/icons/. Outbound links to official product sites are opened only when you choose to follow them.

6. External links

Records link to official product pages, repositories, documentation, and the DevSpireHub parent site. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. A link is not an endorsement and does not transfer data until you click it.

7. Children

The directory is a general-audience technical reference and is not directed at children. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not operate accounts or store form submissions itself.

8. Your choices

  • Browse the directory without saving anything.
  • Clear saved bookmarks and collections with Clear all on the My Collections page, or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Export a JSON backup before clearing or switching browsers.
  • Avoid including sensitive personal information in form messages.

9. Changes to this policy

If the site's data practices change (for example, if a delivery key or a new integration is added), this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. The static site has no mailing list for policy notices, so the page itself is the authoritative record.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data-practice corrections can be sent through the contact page. When form delivery is not configured, the same page links to the DevSpireHub contact channel instead of pretending to send a message.
