Contact

Contact DevSpire Nexus

Corrections, privacy questions, and directory feedback. Messages are delivered through Web3Forms only when the deployer has configured an access key.

settings

Form delivery is not configured in this copy

No Web3Forms access key is present in data/site-config.js, so this form will validate your message but cannot transmit it. You can reach the parent brand through the DevSpireHub contact page.

Send a message

Required fields are marked. Do not include passwords, private keys, or anything you would not publish.

20–2000 characters. For corrections, include the record name, URL, and a public evidence link.

Delivery status is checked in the browser before sending.

When configured, messages are transmitted by Web3Forms to the directory maintainers. See the Privacy Policy.

What to include

  • checkThe record name and the registry page that lists it.
  • checkA public source that supports the correction, such as official documentation.
  • checkWhat specifically is wrong or outdated.
  • blockNo confidential material, credentials, or private personal data.

How messages are handled

Corrections are reviewed manually against the evidence you provide. A message does not automatically change a record, and delivery does not guarantee a response or a publication date.

Other channels

For parent-brand enquiries, use the DevSpireHub website. For catalog submissions, use the structured form.
